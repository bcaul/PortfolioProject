RFL (Robot Fighting League)

RFL is a project that I lead for my university's robotics society. It is one of the society's beginner projects and is the second largest project we offer. It involves teaching students how to built their own battlebot, all the way from ideation to a finished project ready to fight other bots in a competition.

As the project lead I am responsible for:

• Teaching CAD
• Teaching students how to solder and wire their components.
• Teaching students how to 3D print their own designs.
